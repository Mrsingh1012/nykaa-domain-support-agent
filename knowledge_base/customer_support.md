# Customer Support Policy

## Contacting Customer Support

Customers should use the official customer support
options available through the retailer's official
website or mobile application.

## Information to Keep Ready

Before contacting support, customers should prepare:

- Order ID
- Registered email address or phone number
- A clear description of the issue
- Relevant order or payment details

## Order Issues

For delayed deliveries, missing products, damaged
items, returns, or refunds, customers should provide
their order ID and explain the issue clearly.

## Important Notice

Do not use unverified phone numbers, email addresses,
or links.

Always verify current contact details through
the retailer's official channels.
